In front of

Philip Nathanael is a SAG-AFTRA actor born in Guatemala and raised in the U.S., Ecuador, and Peru. He started learning his craft in theater, touring the country for two years with the Covenant Players before moving to Los Angeles in 2007.

He later trained in Meisner technique and also in Choiceless Awareness under Steve Eastin, a student of John Cassavetes.

His years spent operating camera on feature sets have given him something few actors have: a practical understanding of framing and focus that informs how he moves within a shot.

His screen credits include featured roles in the WWII dramas Alone We Fight (Corbin Bernsen, Johnny Messner) and D-Day (Randy Couture, Chuck Liddell), scenes with Michael Madsen, and the fan-funded live-action Scooby-Doo pilot Mystery Incorporated.
