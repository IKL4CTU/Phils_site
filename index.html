02 / About

Behind the
camera.

Philip Nathanael makes films to find out what's possible. His first short, Dreams of Solace (2011), ran twenty minutes without a word of dialogue, a test of whether he could tell a story using only image and performance. He made Double Agent (2017) entirely by himself, with no crew at all: he wrote it, set up every shot, directed, and played both leads, a pair of twins. In Time (2019) was his first film directing a full crew and was selected to screen at the Collaboration Filmmakers Challenge that year.

The experiments change, but the purpose does not. Philip's dream is to tell stories with a message that viewers can apply to their own lives in a positive way.

Combining his experience as an actor and camera operator, he directs with a working knowledge of both sides of the lens and can communicate effectively with the talent and the crew. Everyone hears the vision in a language they understand.

Philip's spirit of experimentation now leads him to virtual reality. He is developing The Framed VR Experience, an approach to narrative VR that keeps the presence of standing inside a scene while restoring the focus that makes cinema work. The goal is not to change how stories are told, but how they are experienced.
